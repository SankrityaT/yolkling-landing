# Yolkling: Don't Torch Our Karma — Sentiment, Authenticity, No-AI Positioning

## The one principle
Every community here punishes the same thing: **treating people as an audience instead of a community.** Be present, give more than you take, let the work prove what it is, tell your story in plain human language. Yolkling's no-AI / code-drawn / bootstrapped / personal-origin story is genuinely unusual and good. It does not need to be SOLD, it needs to be TOLD once, then demonstrated.

## Landmine table (per channel)
- **Reddit (cozy/indie/wellness):** LOVE devlogs, WIP GIFs, genuine questions, behind-the-scenes, milestone-as-story. HATE fresh-account link drops, same trailer cross-posted same day, "wellness app that fixes your mental health" spam (r/selfimprovement, r/getdisciplined hypersensitive), AI images (subs banning AI ~doubled 2023-2024), corporate tone. RULE: never post from a fresh/all-promo profile; mods check history. ~73% still apply the 10% rule, some 99/1.
- **X (gamedev/cozy):** LOVE #screenshotsaturday, art-process video, raw mechanic GIFs, founder vulnerability, celebrating others, no-AI seal posts. HATE "Excited to announce", context-free links, AI art in a hand-drawn crowd, press-release tone. RULE: links in body are deprioritized; build real peer presence for weeks before promo.
- **Hacker News:** LOVE technical specificity ("drew every asset in SwiftUI, no images"), failure stories with data, inspectable demos. HATE waitlist-only Show HN (needs something tryable), hype, marketing titles. RULE: Show HN needs a runnable TestFlight; pre-launch = a technical "how I built X" post or Ask HN instead.
- **TikTok (cozy/wellness/witchtok):** LOVE 15-30s of ONE satisfying mechanic, code-drawing BTS, "day in the life of a solo dev", trending audio + peaceful footage. HATE slow intros (hook in 1-2s or gone), corporate trailers (read as ads), AI visuals in wellness/witchtok, bought engagement. RULE: hook in the first 1-2 seconds; show the creature not the concept. (Finch's top TikTok hit 63.4M views; positioned as a cozy GAME that's good for you, not a mental-health product.)
- **Discord (indie/cozy/self-care servers):** LOVE participating first then sharing a GIF for feedback, beta keys as thanks, AMAs. HATE link-and-disappear, cross-posting identical messages (mods talk), DM-bombing, banned-AI art. RULE: never join a server just to post your link.

## No-AI as a strength (lean in, don't preach)
Context: the "No Gen AI" seal is spreading on Steam/itch; the Indie Game Awards disqualified Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 (Dec 2025) for gen-AI use; Project Zomboid got backlash over suspected AI art. The community actively hunts AI use.
Yolkling: every pixel is SwiftUI code, no image assets — not just "no AI art" but a genuinely unusual technical story.
- Reddit: show the actual SwiftUI path code; let readers conclude "no AI" themselves.
- X: timelapse/before-after of writing the drawing code; the yolk shape next to its path code is instantly shareable.
- TikTok: 20s of the Xcode canvas + code, caption "every line of this creature is written, not drawn." Don't say "AI" at all.
- HN: a technical post "I drew a full iOS creature game entirely in SwiftUI code, no image assets."
- DON'T write "unlike AI slop" / "this is real art." Say the principle ONCE (About / founding post), then never again. The work carries it.

## Authenticity playbook
- **Warm up 2-4 weeks giving only** before any promo (answer questions, recommend others' games, help debug, reply to devs, share their work).
- **Founder story framing:** lead with WHY (a person), not WHAT (a category). A pitch says "here's what it does"; a story says "here's what happened to me and what I made."
- **Disclosure:** "I made this / I'm the dev" near the top, briefly, then the actual content. NO CTAs/links in community post bodies (link lives in bio; people click bios).
- Never: same content to 2+ communities same day; first post on an account being promo; second-account replies/upvotes; asking friends to upvote. Celebrate others' launches often.

## Founder story angles (seeds, no em dashes)
1. **Burnout:** "I was tracking everything wrong: streaks, points, checkboxes, and still burning out... I wanted a tiny creature that just needed me to take care of myself, and took care of me back. No streaks. No failure states."
2. **Technical obsession:** "I got obsessed with a constraint: what if I drew everything in code? No image files, no AI, no stock assets. Every curve, every blink is a SwiftUI path. When you write every line, the creature feels more alive."
3. **Loneliness:** "Sometimes the problem isn't your system, it's that you're doing it alone. Yolkling is company. You sleep, it grows. You're struggling, there's something there waiting. A small witness to the ordinary days."
4. **Childhood + twist:** "I grew up with Neko Atsume and a Tamagotchi. They didn't need me to be anything, they just showed up. I built something like that for the part of adult life that actually needs tending."

## Red-flag phrases to NEVER use
"Excited to announce", "We're proud to share", "Check it out at [link]" in a community post, "Our app helps you", "Download now / Available on the App Store / Sign up for the waitlist" as a CTA, "Game-changing / Revolutionary / Transform your life", "Join our community / Follow us for updates", any unbacked mental-health claim, "Unlike AI-generated games, ours is hand-crafted", "As a solo dev it's been a journey".

## What DOES land
Specific numbers ("three weeks on the idle blink"), "I built this because..." + a real reason, "curious what you think about...", naming the creature like it's real, "no image assets, everything is SwiftUI code", responding to criticism warmly, sharing a failure with no lesson attached, crediting games that inspired you, short human sentences, no em dashes, no jargon.
