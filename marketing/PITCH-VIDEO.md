# the 15-second pitch video

The one asset two separate reviewers have now asked for: a short as-is / to-be
video. Life without the app, life with it, the creature carrying the feeling.
Use it for the landing hero (muted autoplay), TikTok/Reels/Shorts, Product Hunt
gallery, and the App Store preview.

Rule from CLAIMS.md applies here too: nothing in the video the app doesn't do.

## the arc (as-is → turn → to-be)

**AS-IS (0:00–0:04), the problem, no app yet.**
Phone POV, dark room, doomscroll blur. Reels flick past too fast to read.
On-screen text, one line at a time, mono caps:
> 11:47 PM. STILL SCROLLING.

**TURN (0:04–0:07), the hatch.**
Hard cut to the warm eggshell world. The egg wobbles, cracks, bursts, the
creature pops out (capture: onboarding `YOLK_ONB=hatching` seam, or screen-record
the real hatch). Text:
> MEET THE LITTLE GUY WHO'S SECRETLY YOU

**TO-BE (0:07–0:13), the mechanic in two beats.**
Beat 1, rough: creature dim, half-lidded eyes, z z Z floating (app: rough-night
state / landing demo "slept rough"). Text:
> SLEPT 4 HOURS. SO DID HE.
Beat 2, thriving: cut to the creature bright, glowing, bouncing, hearts
(app: `YOLK_HEALTH=high` home state or landing "slept great"). Text:
> ONE WALK. LOOK AT HIM.

**CLOSE (0:13–0:16).**
Creature waves (trust-stage wave, `YOLK_TRUST=1` seam). Logo + line:
> he grows when you do. — yolkling

## voice notes
- lowercase, cozy, no exclamation points, no em dashes
- never show the creature sick, crying, or dying. rough = sleepy. that IS the pitch:
  the only pet that doesn't guilt you
- sound: soft ambient + one satisfying pop at the hatch. captions always on
  (most feeds are muted)

## the persona variant (30s, for PH / a founder post)
Same arc but narrated, "Anna" style, first person:
"i checked my screen time and it said 6 hours. so i built a little creature
that's fed by the opposite: my sleep, my steps, the hours i'm NOT on my phone.
when i live badly he just gets sleepy and waits for me. no guilt, no streak
funerals. he grows when i do."
