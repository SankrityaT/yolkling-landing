# Yolkling Waitlist Funnel (turn Reddit/X traffic into signups)

## Landing page checklist (above the fold = one promise, one input, no exits)
- **Headline** (<12 words, one emotional outcome): e.g. "a little creature that grows when you take care of yourself."
- **Subhead** (one sentence): "steps, sleep, and daily check-ins help your yolk grow and trust you."
- **Single CTA**: ONE email field + one button. No name/phone/"how'd you hear" (each extra field ~ -10%). Button: **"claim my founding species"** (uses the hook; beats "subscribe").
- **Visual**: a 3-5s autoplay silent LOOP of the yolk reacting to care (not a static screenshot). Hand-drawn art front and center.
- **Social proof** beside the field: live counter once 200+, or "only [N] founding species, gone after launch."
- **Cut**: nav menu, "about us"/story above fold, multiple CTAs, long feature lists, "coming soon" as headline, anything that requires scrolling to reach the email field.
- **Below fold (max 3)**: how it works (3-step), founding-member unlock, privacy ("no spam, one email when you're in").

## Incentives that convert (ranked)
1. **Position + queue movement** (Robinhood: 1M signups, ~3x viral coeff) — show position, make it moveable.
2. **Founding species** (Yolkling's best card) — product-tied, exclusive, gone after launch. Name it + show the art + a hard cap with a depleting counter. Confirmation page: "this is yours. nobody after you gets this one."
3. **Founding badge** (permanent in-app, visible when friends visit your room).
4. **Early access** (2 weeks before public, with a hard date).
5. **Tiered referral unlocks** (see below).
Don't bother: generic discounts, vague "exclusive features", "be the first to know" as the only hook.

## Referral loop (ship the simplest this week: unlock-for-invites, Harry's model)
Use GetWaitlist / KickoffLabs / Prefinery / Viral Loops (free tiers, <1 day to wire). Tiers:
- 1 referral: move up 50 spots
- 3: exclusive founding room decoration
- 5: exclusive yolk cosmetic (never in the regular shop)
- 10: "Founding Council" (name in credits + earliest beta + a feedback call)
Must-haves: unique referral URL on the CONFIRMATION page (not just email) + in the welcome email (one-click copy) + visible progress + dup/IP fraud guard.
Expect: 15-35% share at least once; K-factor usually 0.2-0.6 (>1 is rare); referrals = 20-30% of signups when working (Harry's 77% is an outlier).

## Don't-go-cold email nurture (8-12 emails, every 1-2 weeks; never >2/week)
1. **Confirmation** (instant): "your [founding creature] is waiting" + the art + referral link + tiers. (60-80% open)
2. **Referral nudge** (day 3-5): "you're #N in line, here's how to jump" + a copy-paste share message.
3-5. **Dev updates** (every 2 wks): BTS art/animation + a design story; founder story; a never-shown feature reveal (insiders).
6. **Survey** (wk 6-8): one question ("which room would you decorate first?") + reward for responding.
7. **Referral re-engage** (wk 8-10): "you're X referrals from unlocking Y."
8-9. **Countdown** (2 wks / 1 wk out): the date, the access waves.
10. **Launch day**: one link, what to download, what happens to their founding creature.
11. **Post-launch** (day 3 + 7 for non-converters): social proof + one-tap download.
Watch unsubscribe >2%/email = over-emailing.

## Conversion reality (ESTIMATES, flag as such)
- Cold Reddit/X post → landing click: 0.5-3%.
- Landing → signup, cold: 4-12% (4% pure cold, 10-12% if the GIF lands + founding hook is prominent).
- Landing → signup, warm (your followers/Discord): 8-20%+ (personal X audiences ~8.2% vs cold PH 1.4%).
- Referral visitor → signup: 15-25%.
- Waitlist → active at launch: 5-15% consumer (push to the high end with 8+ nurture emails).
- **Practical:** ~500 redditors see a post → ~10-25 click → ~1-3 sign up. A 1,000-person organic waitlist is a multi-MONTH effort, not a week. The referral mechanic is what makes it compound instead of linear.

## Highest-leverage single move (both platforms)
Post the hand-drawn GIF of the yolk reacting to being cared for, NO marketing language, link in bio + pinned reply. Let the emotional pull do the work.
