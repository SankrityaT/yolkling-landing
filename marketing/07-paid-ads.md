# Yolkling Paid Ads + ASO (concrete deliverables)

Built from the verified ad research (deep-research run, June 2026) and the reusable `indie-app-paid-ads` skill. Two parts: the launch-day ASO package, and the pre-launch Meta test brief. Standing rules apply: no founder face in any creative, the US-citizen partner holds the Apple/ad accounts and money, no em dashes, no AI tells, no AI-generated visuals.

---

## PART 1 - App Store Optimization (use at launch)

ASO is free and the single biggest launch lever. Lock this metadata before spending a cent on ads.

### Title / Subtitle / Keywords

**App Name (30 chars):** `Yolkling: Cozy Virtual Pet`  (26/30)
Indexes: yolkling, cozy, virtual, pet. Apple treats the colon as a keyword separator.

**Subtitle (30 chars):** `Gentle self-care companion`  (26/30)
Indexes: gentle, self, care, companion. No words repeated from the title.

**Keywords field (100 chars):**
`calm,mood,journal,mindful,wellbeing,habit,routine,creature,kawaii,wholesome,relax,cute,hatch,grow`  (97/100)
Single words, no spaces, no plurals, nothing repeated from title/subtitle, no "app/free", no competitor brand names (Tamagotchi, Finch, Neko Atsume are trademarked and will get the keyword field rejected, so they are deliberately absent; "virtual pet" in the title captures that search intent instead).

**Dedup check:** title/subtitle words (yolkling, cozy, virtual, pet, gentle, self, care, companion) appear nowhere in the keyword field. No wasted characters.

**Search combinations this assembles** (Apple mixes words across all three fields):
cozy virtual pet, virtual pet, cute pet, cute creature, kawaii pet, gentle pet, self care companion, self care pet, calm companion, mood journal, mindful journal, wellbeing companion, habit pet, routine companion, grow pet, hatch pet, cozy creature, wholesome pet.

**Category:** primary = Health & Fitness (matches the self-care positioning, is one of the cheaper Apple Search Ads categories at ~$3.83 US median CPI vs ~$12.28 for Games, and is exactly where Finch sits). Optional secondary = Lifestyle.
- If you ever select a Games category instead, the genre searchers type "pet game" / "virtual pet game", so swap `wholesome` for `game` in the keyword field. Do not do both categories as Games; the wellness framing converts the cold browser better here.

**Brand defense (do at launch):** the moment you are live, set up a tiny Apple Search Ads campaign bidding only on "yolkling" so no competitor can buy your name and skim the traffic your organic and TikTok push creates. Brand cost-per-tap is very low. A few dollars a day.

### Screenshots (the first 3 are the ones that convert)

Only the first 3 screenshots show in iOS search results, so they carry the load. Lead with the creature and the promise, not a feature grid. Warm hand-drawn palette, consistent across all five, frameless full-bleed so the yolk pops in the thumbnail. Captions lowercase to match the brand, each under 7 words.

1. **HERO** - caption: `a creature that grows with you`. Visual: the yolk at its most charming, full personality, warm cream background. This is the brand in one frame.
2. **CORE LOOP** - caption: `sleep, water, sunlight, it lights up`. Visual: the daily check-in with the yolk visibly responding. Shows how it works.
3. **DIFFERENTIATOR** - caption: `no streaks. no guilt. ever.`. Visual: the gentle, no-failure-state screen. This is the frame that makes someone pick Yolkling over Finch or a habit tracker.
4. **SOCIAL** - caption: `visit friends, leave little postcards`. Visual: two rooms / a postcard moment. The delight feature and the most shareable one.
5. **IDENTITY + soft CTA** - caption: `every pixel hand-drawn, no AI`. Visual: the yolk next to a glimpse of its SwiftUI path code, or a wall of unique yolks. Closes on the one thing no competitor can copy.

**Important correction:** some ASO guides claim Apple now OCRs screenshot caption text into keyword ranking (a 2025 rumor). The research refuted this 3-0. Screenshots drive **conversion, not ranking.** Write captions to sell, not to stuff keywords.

### App preview video (optional but high-value)

15 to 30s. First 3 seconds must be the yolk doing something charming (the hook). No founder face. No AI voice (the brand is no-AI). Arc: hatch, then a care moment, then the yolk reacting, then the social postcard beat. **This same clip is your Meta and TikTok ad creative.** Build it once, use it in three places.

## PART 2 - Pre-launch Meta test (use NOW, $200/mo)

Goal: cheap waitlist email signups to yolkling.com, and a validated hook to run for free on organic. At this budget this is a creative lab, not a growth campaign.

### Setup (who and what)

- The **US-citizen partner** owns the Meta Business account and payment method. The founder can operate day to day with access granted, writes the copy, but holds no payment method and signs nothing. Nothing in the ad implies the founder runs a US business or takes payments.
- One campaign, **Leads objective** (optimize for email signup), pointed at yolkling.com (or a Meta lead form if simpler).
- **Targeting:** broad, US, ages 18-40, plus one interest layer (cozy games, virtual pet, journaling, mindfulness; you can name Finch / Neko Atsume as *interests* even though you cannot use them as ASO keywords). iOS users.
- **Budget pacing:** $5 to $10/day for 2 to 3 weeks. That is the whole $200. Do not raise it until one hook clearly wins.

### Creative (faceless, four hooks, one clip)

Use the yolk preview clip. Four ad variants = the **same clip, four different opening lines / text overlays**, identical targeting, so the hook is the only variable. All lowercase, no em dashes, no AI tells:

1. `your little guy gets sleepy when you doomscroll` (the signature mechanic, most scroll-stopping)
2. `i made an app that wants you to put your phone down` (the contrarian hook)
3. `no streaks. no guilt. a tiny creature that's just glad you showed up` (the anti-streak / anti-Finch hook)
4. `take care of yourself and watch it grow. that's the whole thing.` (the plain promise)

Faceless formats to shoot (pick one or mix): the creature in motion (this alone is the ad), hands-and-screen (thumb scrolling the app), or text-on-screen over the clip. Hook lands in the first 1 to 2 seconds or the view is gone.

### Read the result

- Expected: roughly **$1 to $2 per signup** (so ~100 to 200 signups on $200), and one hook with a clearly lower cost per signup.
- Decision rule: after a variant has spent ~$30 to $40 (or ~50 link clicks), if its cost per signup is more than ~2x the best variant, pause it.
- If **every** variant is above ~$2.50 per signup, the creative is the problem, not the budget. Pause, rework the clip and hooks, test again. Do not scale a losing creative.
- The payoff: take the cheapest-cost hook and run it for **free** as your main TikTok, Reddit, and X angle. That reuse is the real return on this spend.

### What not to do at this budget

Reddit Ads (below its testing floor), Google Ads (no install yet, no search intent), paid ASO agencies, and cheap-CPI install services. All organic-only or skip.
