# Yolkling Elevator Pitch + Copy (no em dashes)

## Framework
- **Problem / solution / why-us** — for people who need convincing (investors, press).
- **"X for Y" one-liner** — for fast recall (social, App Store, word of mouth).
- **Value-first / outcome lead** — for warm audiences (landing/waitlist copy).

## One-liner (<10 words)
1. **a tiny creature that grows when you do** ← top pick (natural double meaning)
2. your yolk thrives when you take care of yourself
3. the little pet that rewards being kind to yourself

## Tweet-length (~20-25 words)
1. yolkling is a cozy creature companion that warms up to you the more you sleep, move, and check in on yourself. no guilt, just warmth.
2. **take care of yourself, watch your yolk grow. yolkling is a gentle virtual creature tied to your sleep, steps, and daily check-ins.** ← top pick ("no streaks. no pressure." earns trust fast)
3. meet your yolk. a hand-drawn little creature that gets cozier and closer the more you actually look after yourself. no streaks. no pressure.

## "X for Y" positioning
1. **neko atsume for people building a gentler relationship with themselves** ← top pick
2. tamagotchi for your actual wellbeing, without the guilt trip
3. a cozy room game where the character you grow is you

## 30-second spoken pitch
**Top pick (A):** "yolkling is a cozy iOS app where you raise a little creature called a yolk. the yolk warms up to you over time, but the way you grow that bond is by taking care of yourself in real life. sleep, steps, focus sessions, daily check-ins. the more you look after yourself, the more your yolk opens up. you decorate its room, collect colours, send postcards to friends. no streaks. totally hand-drawn. think neko atsume crossed with a self-care journal. waitlist is open at yolkling.com."

## Locked voice for all marketing copy
gentle, lowercase-ok, "tiny / little creature / just checking in". NO em dashes. Never "revolutionize / supercharge / reimagine". Always close the objections: no streaks, no guilt, hand-drawn, no AI.
