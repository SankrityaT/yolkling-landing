# Yolkling X / Twitter Growth Playbook

## Highest-leverage post formats
1. **Native GIF / video demo** (~10x reach of text; never link out to YouTube). Looping yolk reacting to you, <15s.
2. **"Why I made this" origin story** (3-5 post thread; founder vulnerability > success content).
3. **Before/after progress GIF** (rewarded on #screenshotsaturday; proves human craft).
4. **Relatable self-care hook tweet** (replies weighted 27x a like; drives the target demo).
5. **Build-in-public milestone** ("100 on the waitlist", "47 frames for one blink").
6. **Handmade / no-AI transparency post** — 52% of devs now view genAI negatively (up from 18%). "Every pixel drawn by hand in SwiftUI" is a genuine differentiator + community magnet.
7. **Poll / community question** (engagement + free market research).

## Hashtags (2-3 max per post, rotate)
#indiedev, #gamedev, #screenshotsaturday (Saturdays only, with GIF), #buildinpublic, #wholesomegames / #wholesome, #cozygames, #indiegame(s), #selfcare, #virtualPet, #SwiftUI.

## Rituals / communities
- **Screenshot Saturday** (every Sat, GIF) — do not miss. Bank 3-4 assets ahead.
- **#IndieDevHour**, **#WIPWednesday**, **Build-in-Public Fridays**.
- Amplifier accounts: **@wholesomegames**, **@wholesomesnk**, **@TweetIndieGames**, **@cozygamingclub**, **@cozycelebration**. Tag when genuinely relevant.

## Algorithm reality
- First 30-60 min decides distribution; dead after 24h.
- Engagement weights vs a like: reply 27x, reply-chain-you-respond 150x, QT 25x, RT 20x, profile click 12x, bookmark 10x.
- **External links suppressed** — put yolkling.com in bio; in tweets say "link in bio" or drop it in a self-reply.
- Native media > linked (~40% more engagement).
- **Cadence:** 4-5 posts/week + 10-15 genuine replies/day. Consistent times (9am-12pm audience tz). "Reply guy" strategy: reply early to 5K-50K-follower cozy/indie/wellness accounts.

## Landmines
- AI-art suspicion (highest risk) — be explicit + early, show process/code/frame counts.
- Over-promotion without relationship — 80/20, 2 weeks reply-only first.
- External links killing reach — link in bio.
- Inauthentic corporate voice — write small + human; "tiny", "a little creature", "just checking in".
- Consistency gaps — bank assets.
